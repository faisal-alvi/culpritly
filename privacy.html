The Culpritly product

Culpritly is in early access. When you connect a store, the connector plugin is designed to send only what is needed to detect and explain checkout breakage:

  • Change events: plugin, theme and core updates, activations, and changes to key WooCommerce and payment settings, with timestamps.
  • Checkout errors: JavaScript error messages from the checkout page and payment gateway failure messages.
  • Order counts and totals, used to learn your store's normal pace and estimate revenue impact.

Culpritly never collects card data. It does not collect customer personal data beyond what is needed to do the job. A full product privacy policy and data processing terms will be published before the product leaves early access.
